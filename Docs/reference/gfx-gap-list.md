## Reference notes (web)

- RR3's engine adds per-pixel shadows "so that passing under a tree or a building causes that shadow to be cast on the
  car", and spherical-harmonics night lighting (EA, "Under the Hood: How Real Racing 3 Gets Its Motor Running",
  https://www.ea.com/news/under-the-hood-how-real-racing-3-gets-its-motor-running). Firemonkeys describe more texture
  layers, more normal maps on cars, more per-pixel lighting and "better dynamic reflections" on newer devices
  (Pocket Gamer interview with Rob Murray, https://www.pocketgamer.com/real-racing-3/firemonkeys-rob-murray-on-how-real-racing-3-offers-a-generational-jump-in-visual/).
  For us that means: the trackside in the car's reflections (item 6) and the tree/stand shade on the car body (check
  `_CarLit` under the RoadShadow mask) are the two car-side gaps.
- Monza 2024 refurbishment: the whole lap resurfaced (Pirelli: darker tarmac), kerbs replaced or lowered, new gravel
  traps at the Rettifilo and the Roggia and a wider asphalt area at the Rettifilo (https://news.gp/en/monzas-upgrades-what-new-2024-italian-grand-prix).
  Ascari keeps gravel run-off; the Parabolica's outside is tarmac (F1 circuit guide,
  https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/circuit-guide-everything-you-need-to-know-about-the-autodromo-nazionale-monza.51PKqBRlxNs0fzLWQzsnd0.51PKqBRlxNs0fzLWQzsnd0).
  Items 7 and 13 should follow that (gravel beyond a narrow asphalt band at the chicanes, darker fresh asphalt).
