Notes
- Drivers and pit crew are original (CC0). Body shape, rig and weights come from the MakeHuman / MPFB2 asset data (CC0 1.0, see LICENSE.ASSETS.md in that repository). No MakeHuman or MPFB program code was used or shipped. Suits, gloves, boots, helmets and all their textures were generated procedurally for this project.
- Fans, officials, media, engineers and marshals are Microsoft Rocketbox avatars (MIT licence), converted for this project: highest LOD only, bones renamed to Mixamo names (facial bones merged into Head), materials atlased to one texture set, vertex colours removed, hair kept as alpha-clip cards. Edits: marshals recoloured to bright orange with painted gloves, "PARAMEDIC" lettering and badges removed, a hard hat and tool belt removed; engineers' shirts made team-tintable with a radio headset added; camera and microphone props added. Texels outside the UV islands were blanked, which removes the source watermarks. Previews were checked for brands, logos and text.
- The MIT licence requires its notice to ship with the avatars. Keep the text below (also in humans/LICENSE_rocketbox.md) in the game's credits or third-party notices.
- No real people's faces: Rocketbox faces are synthetic library characters.
- Tint mask: white = main team colour, grey 128 = accent, black = keep as painted. Tinted areas are painted light grey in the albedo.
- Normal maps are OpenGL (Y+). Roughness maps are greyscale; the GLB uses them as the glTF metallicRoughness texture with metallic 0.
- GLB: Y-up, facing +Z, feet at Y=0, metres, one material (alpha clip at 0.5, double-sided). Textures are NOT embedded: each GLB references the PNGs next to it by file name, so keep each character's folder together. Unity glTFast and Blender load this directly.
- Texture sizes: drivers 2048, crew 1024, Rocketbox characters 1024 albedo/tint with 512 normal and roughness.
- _source/ holds the generator and converter scripts (Blender 5.3 bpy, Python). _previews/ holds JPEG renders (Cycles; hair shown without self-shadowing, as on a mobile renderer). They are not needed in the game build.
